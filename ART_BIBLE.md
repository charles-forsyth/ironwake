# IRONWAKE art bible (v1, 2026-11-08)

Look: A (stormy grey ironclads) + airships. Reference: looks/look_a.png.

## Mood
Cold, heavy, maritime. North-Atlantic weather on an invented archipelago: slate seas, low cloud,
spray, soot. Beauty comes from weather and light, not colour. Sun breaks through rarely and matters
when it does. Night actions: searchlights, muzzle flash, fires.

## Palette
- World: slate grey #3E464D, sea green-grey #2F3D3A, foam #D8DEDF, cliff basalt #2A2A2C, wet moss #4B5A3A.
- Ships: riveted iron #4A4E52, gunmetal #2E3236, soot black #1A1A1B, brass #B08A3E (sparingly),
  red-oxide boot topping #6B2A22 at the waterline, weathered white superstructure #C9C6BC.
- Accent (rare): furnace/aether orange #FF7A1A for fires, boiler glow, tesla arcs. Under 3% of any frame.
- Factions by trim colour only (stripe/flag/funnel band), never by hull colour:
  League gold #C9A24A, Iron Navy blue-grey #5B7088, Free Captains oxblood #7A2430, Tech Order verdigris #4F8C7E.

## Materials
Riveted iron plates with seams and rust streaks, brass fittings, soot gradient on funnels, salt
crust at the waterline, canvas and rope on airships, worn wood decks on small craft. Everything
weathered; nothing showroom-clean. Real-world PBR ranges (roughness iron 0.5-0.7, brass 0.3-0.45).

## Silhouettes and scale (real metres)
- Sea: cutter 25 m, gunboat 45 m, steam frigate 80 m, cruiser 120 m, ironclad 140 m, dreadnought 180 m.
  Read at a glance by funnel count and turret layout: low hulls, tall funnels, big turrets.
- Air (airships, steampunk not sci-fi): rigid envelopes with ribbed fabric, gondolas of riveted iron,
  side propellers, trailing cables. Scout airship 60 m, gunship 110 m, carrier-dreadnought airship 220 m
  (rare, faction capital). They fly low (150-600 m), drift with wind, and are slow and vulnerable.
- Airship role: scouting (reveal fog of war), bombing runs on slow ships, spotting for long-range
  gunnery, and anti-air guns/flak on ships as an upgrade slot. Late game: own airship escort.

## World
Basalt sea stacks, lighthouses, coaling stations, fortified harbours with cranes and gas lamps.
Built from Fab/Quixel scans only (cliffs, rocks, beach, docks). No stylized props.

## Budgets
Hero ship 150-300k tris with Nanite (Nanite fine for hulls), turrets/funnels/masts as separate
meshes for damage states. NPC ships share the same models. Airships ~100-200k tris.

## Concept prompt rules
- Concept sheets for image-to-3D: single vessel, orthographic side + top + front, plain light-grey
  background, no text, no labels, no water.
- Mood shots: 16:9, overcast, include sea spray and scale cues (gulls, small boats, cliffs).
