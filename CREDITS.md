### Generated Steampunk Fleet, Buildings & Crew
- Tall Brig hull (`42 m`), coastal steamer, pilot cutter, harbour tug, paddle steamer, salvage tender, rowing gig, patrol & cargo airships, harbour infrastructure (steam crane, coaling station, signal tower, steam buoy, mooring mast, gas lamp, bollard/capstan, dock warehouse), town buildings, gulls, and 1870s sailor crew: generated from project concept art via **Hunyuan3D-2** (Tencent) and rigged with procedural articulated parts in Blender (`pipeline/*.py`).
