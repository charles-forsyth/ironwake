# Support

## Getting Help

- **Interactive Showcase & 3D Drydock:** Explore the ships, ports, audio stems, and Havelock wake physics directly in your browser at <https://charles-forsyth.github.io/ironwake/>.
- **Architecture & Controls:** Read [README.md](README.md) and [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) (plus the 15-chapter verified technical reference in [`spec/`](spec/)).

- **Security Issues:** Please use [Private Vulnerability Reporting](SECURITY.md) rather than public issues.
